Frequently Asked Questions

How do I submit a request?

Sign in and choose Submit a Request. Describe the problem, including the affected device or service and any error message. The system sets the priority automatically and suggests a category.

How will I know when someone replies?

When a technician or administrator replies, or the status of your request changes, a number appears next to Notifications in the menu. Select a notification to open the ticket.

What do the ticket statuses mean?

New: received. Assigned: a technician is responsible. In Progress: being worked on. Waiting for User: we need information from you. Resolved: a solution has been provided. Closed: no further action.

The ticket says "Waiting for User". What should I do?

Open the ticket and reply in the Correspondence section. Your reply returns the ticket to the technician automatically.

How is my ticket's priority decided?

Every ticket gets a priority automatically from what you write, using the AI model and keyword rules (for example, an outage affecting everyone is Critical). Support staff can adjust it after review.

Should I include my password in a ticket?

No. Support staff will never ask for your password or verification codes.
